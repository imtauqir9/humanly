# LinkedIn performance, SEO and GEO demand for AI networking / AI infrastructure / agentic AI topics (state as of October 2026)

Reading notes for the writer:
- "MEASURED" = dataset study with a stated sample. "VENDOR" = tool-vendor blog, opinion or unsourced claim. "BACKGROUND" = data from 2025 or earlier.
- All figures were taken from page summaries retrieved on 2026-10-02; I did not open the gated PDFs (van der Blom, SignalScout, DSMN8), so their detailed tables are not captured here.
- Most LinkedIn studies are published by vendors selling LinkedIn tools. Their samples are their own customers, which skews toward active marketers and company pages.

## 1. How does the LinkedIn algorithm treat content formats in 2026 (reach, dwell, length, hooks, cadence, links)?

### Takeaway
Across the three large 2026 datasets, document carousels and multi-image posts lead on engagement, video volume is up while per-video views are down, and plain link posts are last. Reach per post has been falling since 2025, medium-length text (roughly 150-250 words) currently gets the best reach, and posting 2-5 times a week is the best-evidenced cadence threshold.

### Cited Findings

Format performance (MEASURED, 2026 publications)
- Socialinsider, 1.3M posts from 16,645 business pages, Jan 2024-Dec 2025 (published 16 Mar 2026, updated 15 Jul 2026). Engagement rate by format in 2025: native documents 7.00% (+14% YoY), multi-image 6.45%, video 6.00% (+7%), image 5.30% (+9%), text 4.50% (+12%), poll 4.20%, link 3.25%. Platform average 5.20% (+8% YoY). — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/linkedin-benchmark/)
- Same study: average video views fell 36% YoY across all page sizes (1-5K follower pages: 155 views, down from 190; 100K-1M pages: 1,380, down from 2,430), while pages doubled video output to 4 per month. — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/linkedin-benchmark/)
- Same study: multi-image posts lead impressions for pages under 50K followers; polls lead above 50K. Follower growth rates dropped sharply (1-5K pages: 24.5% vs 40.75% the year before). — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/linkedin-benchmark/)
- Metricool, 673,658 posts from 63,108 accounts, Jan-Feb 2025 vs Jan-Feb 2026 (published 14 Apr 2026): likes -13%, comments -17%, shares -10%, clicks +5%, overall engagement +14% (the "invisible interactions" headline: clicks rather than visible reactions). — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/press-release-linkedin-study-2026/)
- Metricool: personal profiles get 63% higher engagement than company pages; only 7% of company pages grew enough to move up a follower tier; carousels and multi-image posts outperform standard formats; video is now the most-used format on personal profiles yet underperforms carousels, reversing its 2025 trajectory. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/press-release-linkedin-study-2026/)
- A French summary of the Metricool study headlines carousels generating up to 11x the interactions of images (headline only; I did not verify the figure in the primary report). — [J'ai un pote dans la com](https://jai-un-pote-dans-la.com/etude-metricool-engagement-linkedin/)
- DSMN8, 110,000 employee posts, Jan-Feb 2026 (published 14 Apr 2026): "No single format wins across every metric"; reach, engagement rate and clicks each favour different formats. Numeric tables are in a gated PDF. — [DSMN8](https://dsmn8.com/resources/whats-working-on-linkedin-in-2026-report/)
- SignalScout via Koka Sexton, 2,279 posts from 246 B2B creators over 13 months (page last modified 15 Aug 2026): mean 58 engagements per post; top 10% threshold 326+, top 1% 2,000+ (power-law distribution); 86% of engagements are likes, 5% shares; over 90% of engagers on a post are first-time engagers; early discussion beats passive likes. Small sample. — [Koka Sexton / SignalScout](https://kokasexton.com/linkedin-algorithm-report-2026/)

Length and formatting (MEASURED)
- The Shield Index, 167,940 posts, Nov 2025-Feb 2026 (published 5 Apr 2026): engagement rate rises slightly with length (under 75 words 2.23%; 75-150 words 2.20%; 150-250 words 2.34%; 250+ words 2.71%), but reach peaks at 150-175 words. — [The Shield Index](https://theshieldindex.beehiiv.com/p/does-linkedin-post-length-actually-matter)
- Same: the reach advantage of very long posts over short posts shrank from 1.77x (Nov 2025) to 1.32x (Feb 2026); short posts gained 17% reach, very long posts lost 13%. — [The Shield Index](https://theshieldindex.beehiiv.com/p/does-linkedin-post-length-actually-matter)
- Same: length helps text-only posts most (+84% reach from short to very long), barely helps image posts (+12%), and hurts document/carousel posts (long captions on carousels perform worse). — [The Shield Index](https://theshieldindex.beehiiv.com/p/does-linkedin-post-length-actually-matter)
- Same: for 150+ word posts, moderate line breaks (2-5 per 100 words) get the best reach; very "airy" one-line-per-sentence formatting gets lower reach despite higher engagement. Accounts under 25K followers peak at 150-250 words; accounts over 50K peak at 75-150 words. — [The Shield Index](https://theshieldindex.beehiiv.com/p/does-linkedin-post-length-actually-matter)

Cadence (MEASURED, BACKGROUND 2025)
- Buffer, 2M+ posts from 94,000+ accounts, fixed-effects regression controlling for account (published 28 Aug 2025): versus 1 post/week, 2-5 posts/week adds about 1,182 impressions per post and +0.23 pts engagement rate; 6-10 posts/week adds about 5,001; 11+ adds about 16,946. "Moving from 1 post a week to 2 to 5 is like flipping a switch." — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-linkedin/)
- The Buffer page also states carousels generated 278% more engagement than video and 600% more than text. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-linkedin/); Dataslayer presents the same 278% figure as its own internal testing — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/linkedin-algorithm-february-2026-whats-working-now). Attribution conflict; treat the number as a single unverified data point.

Overall reach trend, links, dwell (mostly VENDOR or BACKGROUND)
- Richard van der Blom's Algorithm Insights 2025 report is cited as showing views down 50%, engagement down 25%, follower growth down 59% (BACKGROUND, secondhand). — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/linkedin-algorithm-february-2026-whats-working-now)
- Dataslayer (published 12 Feb 2026, updated Jul 2026) claims external links cut reach by about 60%, new posts are tested on 2-5% of the network, and the first 60 minutes are decisive, attributing these to "LinkedIn official guidance" without linking to a LinkedIn source. Treat as VENDOR claims. — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/linkedin-algorithm-february-2026-whats-working-now)
- Same article's unsourced recommendations: carousels of 5-10 slides, native video of 30-90 seconds, the first roughly 150 characters before "See more" function as the hook, company pages 3-5 posts/week. VENDOR. — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/linkedin-algorithm-february-2026-whats-working-now)
- Measured support for the link penalty direction: link posts have the lowest engagement rate of any format (3.25%) in Socialinsider's data. — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/linkedin-benchmark/)
- Counter-signal: clicks are the one visible interaction that grew in 2026 (+5% YoY). — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/press-release-linkedin-study-2026/)

### Inferences
- For an individual author, the best-supported packaging is a document carousel or multi-image post with a short caption, or a text post of about 150-250 words with moderate line breaks. Long captions on carousels are counterproductive.
- Video should be chosen for the content (demo, walkthrough), not for reach: supply is up and per-video views are down.
- Since over 90% of engagers are new each time and the distribution is power-law, a consistent 2-5 posts/week matters more than optimizing any single post.
- Personal profile, not company page, is the right publishing surface.

### Gaps
- The 2026 edition of van der Blom's Algorithm Insights report was not retrieved; only 2025 figures were found secondhand. Dwell-time thresholds, comment-weighting multipliers and "golden hour" numbers widely quoted online could not be traced to a primary 2026 source.
- No LinkedIn first-party engineering source was retrieved for the link penalty, initial test audience size or the 360Brew ranking model.
- No measured 2026 data found on newsletters/articles reach inside the feed, or on hook wording.
- Hootsuite 2026 data was not retrieved.

## 2. Are LinkedIn posts/articles indexed by Google and cited by AI answer engines, and which traits raise citation likelihood?

### Takeaway
LinkedIn was one of the most-cited domains in AI answers in early 2026, but one large tracker shows its citation share falling about 65% by mid-2026, with ChatGPT nearly dropping it while Google AI Mode and AI Overviews still cite it. Articles are cited and ranked far more than feed posts; keyword-complete headlines, original content, statistics, quotations, answer-first structure and freshness are the best-evidenced traits.

### Cited Findings

LinkedIn in AI answers (MEASURED, 2026)
- Semrush, 325,000 prompts, 89,000 LinkedIn URLs, Jan-Feb 2026 (published 10 Mar 2026): LinkedIn cited in 14.3% of ChatGPT Search responses, 13.5% of Google AI Mode, 5.3% of Perplexity (about 11% average), ranking second among all cited domains. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/linkedin-ai-visibility-study/)
- Same: articles are 50-66% of LinkedIn citations, feed posts 15-28%; 95% of cited items are original content, 5% reshares. Most-cited lengths: articles 500-2,000 words, posts 50-299 words. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/linkedin-ai-visibility-study/)
- Same: cited posts have modest engagement (median 15-25 reactions, at most 1 comment). About 75% of cited authors post 5+ times per 4 weeks; about half have 2,000+ followers, and authors under 500 followers were as likely or more likely to be cited. ChatGPT and AI Mode favour individual creators (59%); Perplexity favours company pages (59%). — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/linkedin-ai-visibility-study/)
- Decline: Cloro, 1,531,027 AI answers across seven engines, Nov 2025-Aug 2026 (published 8 Aug 2026, updated 15 Sep 2026): LinkedIn's citation share fell from 13.8% (Feb 2026) to 4.8% (Jul 2026), and articles from 10.7% to 2.8%. In the 30 days to 7 Aug 2026 LinkedIn was the 14th most-cited domain at 1.61%. — [Cloro](https://cloro.dev/blog/linkedin-articles-vs-posts/)
- Same, LinkedIn article citation rate by engine in that window: Google AI Mode 4.07%, Google AI Overviews 2.18%, Perplexity 1.27%, Copilot 0.54%, ChatGPT 0.06%, Gemini about 0%. Among LinkedIn-citing answers: articles 59.1%, feed posts 33.3%, company pages 4.2%. A brand's own domain accounted for 19.86% of citations. Cloro is a vendor with its own prompt set; the Semrush and Cloro samples are not directly comparable. — [Cloro](https://cloro.dev/blog/linkedin-articles-vs-posts/)
- Profound is also reported as having found LinkedIn a top-cited AI search domain (secondhand, not opened). — [Media Copilot](https://mediacopilot.ai/linkedin-top-cited-ai-search-profound/)

LinkedIn in Google (MEASURED, 2026, small/independent studies)
- Analysis of 1,260 searches and 9,805 LinkedIn URLs, Aug 2026: Pulse articles ranked in Google's top 20 for 46.5% of tested searches, appeared in 30% of AI Overviews and 41% of Perplexity results, but only 1.5% of ChatGPT/Claude answers. — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/linkedin-seo-5-steps-from-1-260-searches-and-9-805-urls-319753c72c)
- Same: only 6.7% of feed posts without backlinks ranked top 20, versus 31.2% with backlinks. Articles whose headline contained every query word ranked 42.1% of the time vs 7.2% with under one-third coverage; query words in the first 50 characters ranked 29.1% vs 16.3% at characters 50-150. Median ranking feed post was 300 days old; only 10% were under three months. Word count, emoji, heading count and paragraph structure did not predict ranking. — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/linkedin-seo-5-steps-from-1-260-searches-and-9-805-urls-319753c72c)
- Conflicting signal: Foundation Inc. data is reported as showing LinkedIn Pulse organic traffic down 89% (33M to 3.6M monthly visits) while standard posts rose 250% to 11M organic visits (secondhand via newsletter, roughly Mar-Apr 2026). — [Neal Schaffer](https://newsletter.nealschaffer.com/posts/linkedin-pulse-lost-89-of-its-google-traffic); original at [Foundation Inc.](https://foundationinc.co/lab/linkedin-pulse-post-traffic) (not opened)

General GEO traits (MEASURED, mostly BACKGROUND 2024-2025, compiled by an agency)
- Princeton/Georgia Tech/IIT/Allen AI GEO paper (KDD 2024): adding statistics raised visibility by about 37-41%, adding quotations by about 28-40%, citing sources by about 8% alone. — [Radiant Elephant compilation](https://www.radiantelephant.com/?p=47908)
- Answer-first structure: 55% of AI Overview citations come from the top 30% of a page (CXL/Kevin Indig, 1.2M citations). — [Radiant Elephant](https://www.radiantelephant.com/?p=47908)
- Freshness: AI-cited content is 25.7% fresher on average; 76.4% of ChatGPT citations were under 30 days old (Ahrefs, 17M citations, 2025). — [Radiant Elephant](https://www.radiantelephant.com/?p=47908)
- Original research earns 4.31x more citations per URL than directories (Yext, 17.2M citations, 2025). Third-party brand mentions correlate 0.664 with AI Overview visibility (Ahrefs, 75K brands, 2025). — [Radiant Elephant](https://www.radiantelephant.com/?p=47908)
- YouTube is the strongest correlating factor for AI Overviews and about 23.3% of AIO citations (Ahrefs/Surfer, 2025). — [Radiant Elephant](https://www.radiantelephant.com/?p=47908)
- 62% of AI citations come from outside Google's top 10 (Ahrefs, 863K SERPs, 2026). Only 11% domain overlap between ChatGPT and Perplexity citations (2025). — [Radiant Elephant](https://www.radiantelephant.com/?p=47908)
- llms.txt showed zero correlation with citations across 300K domains (SE Ranking, 2025). — [Radiant Elephant](https://www.radiantelephant.com/?p=47908)
- Ahrefs 2026 headlines: AI Overviews appear for 83% of branded searches (2 Oct 2026); AI Overviews cut CTR by 23.1% in France (16 Sep 2026); a 331,000-page study found Google does not penalize AI content as such (27 Jul 2026). Titles only, studies not opened. — [Ahrefs blog](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-trends/)

### Inferences
- The two Google findings reconcile if Pulse articles rank well for the queries they target but total Pulse traffic collapsed; either way a feed post alone rarely ranks unless something links to it, and it takes months.
- For AI citation, a LinkedIn article with a headline that states the exact query ("InfiniBand vs Ethernet for AI clusters"), a definition and numbers near the top, is the best-evidenced LinkedIn asset, and its value is now mainly with Google AI Mode/AI Overviews and Perplexity, not ChatGPT.
- Because LinkedIn's citation share proved volatile within six months, mirroring the same explainer on an owned domain and on YouTube is the safer GEO play.
- Follower count and engagement are not prerequisites for being cited; frequency and originality are.

### Gaps
- No independent confirmation of the Cloro decline (for example an updated Semrush or Profound number) was retrieved.
- No study isolates GEO traits specifically for LinkedIn content beyond length and originality; the statistics/quotation effects come from web-page experiments from 2024.
- Copilot data is limited to one vendor figure.

## 3. What search demand evidence exists for the target keywords?

### Takeaway
I found hard keyword volume only for the agent-side terms: "model context protocol" at about 40.5K monthly searches (status "peaked") and "AI agentic" at 3.6K ("exploding"). For the networking terms I found no public volume or Google Trends figures; the available evidence is market data showing Ethernet overtaking InfiniBand, 1.6T shipping and co-packaged optics ramping in 2026.

### Cited Findings

Keyword-tool data
- "Model context protocol": 40.5K monthly searches, +4,400% over 2 years, status "Peaked", volatility high (Exploding Topics; point-in-time tool estimate). — [Exploding Topics](https://explodingtopics.com/topic/model-context-protocol)
- "AI agentic": 3.6K monthly searches, +2,650% over 2 years, status "Exploding". — [Exploding Topics](https://explodingtopics.com/topic/ai-agentic)
- "Enterprise AI agents" is listed among the fastest-growing AI topics of the last two years (+99x, volume not shown). — [Exploding Topics](https://explodingtopics.com/ai-topics-last-2-years-by-growth)

Market evidence as a demand proxy for networking terms
- Dell'Oro (18 Dec 2025, BACKGROUND/forecast): Ethernet surpassed InfiniBand in AI back-end networks during 2025, two years after InfiniBand held nearly 80% of sales. — [Dell'Oro Group](https://www.delloro.com/2026-predictions-data-center-switch-frontend-ai-backed-networks/)
- Dell'Oro: 800G passed 20M ports within three years (400G took 6-7 years); 1.6T switches expected to ship in volume in 2026; 2026 expected to see the initial volume ramp of co-packaged optics on both InfiniBand and Ethernet switches (predictions). — [Dell'Oro Group](https://www.delloro.com/2026-predictions-data-center-switch-frontend-ai-backed-networks/)
- Dell'Oro: Ethernet moving into scale-up against NVLink and UALink, described as potentially "the largest total addressable market expansion the industry has ever seen"; Arista leads Ethernet DC switching; Accton, Celestica and NVIDIA outperformed in 2025; SONiC adoption expected to accelerate. — [Dell'Oro Group](https://www.delloro.com/2026-predictions-data-center-switch-frontend-ai-backed-networks/)
- IEEE ComSoc Technology Blog (31 Jan 2026): Ethernet is a majority of AI back-end switch ports, "trending toward 70-80%" (blog estimate, not a measured figure); IDC 3Q25 ODM-direct data center sales up over 150% YoY; IEEE 802.3dj (200G to 1.6T) completion expected late 2026. — [ComSoc Technology Blog](https://techblog.comsoc.org/2026/01/31/analysis-ethernet-gains-on-infiniband-in-data-center-connectivity-market-white-box-odm-vendors-top-choice-for-ai-hyperscalers/)

### Inferences
- Agent-protocol terms have the large, measurable search volume but MCP is flagged as past peak, so MCP posts need a fresh angle (for example MCP for network operations) rather than "what is MCP".
- Networking terms are low-volume, high-intent B2B queries; "InfiniBand vs Ethernet" has a newsworthy, data-backed storyline (the 80% to minority reversal) that suits a comparison post.
- Combining a high-volume agent term with a niche networking term ("agentic NetOps", "MCP for network automation") is likely the best demand/competition trade-off; this is my inference, not a measured finding.

### Gaps
- No volume, Google Trends or rising-query data found for: "AI networking", "AI data center networking", "InfiniBand vs Ethernet", "Ultra Ethernet", "co-packaged optics", "agentic AI architecture", "AI agent infrastructure", "AI factory", "agent to agent protocol"/A2A, "agentic NetOps". Google Trends cannot be queried through the tools I had, and keyword-tool data is paywalled. Someone with Ahrefs, Semrush or Google Trends access should pull these directly.
- No Ultra Ethernet Consortium adoption data was retrieved.

## 4. Which technical/B2B AI infrastructure posts and creators perform, and what makes writing read as human?

### Takeaway
I found no measured study of AI-infrastructure creators on LinkedIn. What is measured: personal profiles beat company pages, a detector vendor classifies about 81% of longer LinkedIn posts as likely AI-written, and LinkedIn is adding member feedback for "AI slop", so specific first-hand detail is the main differentiator.

### Cited Findings
- Originality.ai, 5,000 public LinkedIn posts of 100+ words, July 2026 (published 30 Jul 2026): 81.2% classified "Likely AI", up from about 50% in late 2024. Detector-vendor study; classifiers have false positives, and the study reports no engagement comparison. — [Originality.ai](https://originality.ai/blog/linkedin-ai-study-engagement)
- Same source reports LinkedIn's Chief Product Officer saying the platform is "ramping the ability for members to tell us" when content looks AI-generated, via a "Seems like AI slop" feedback option. — [Originality.ai](https://originality.ai/blog/linkedin-ai-study-engagement)
- Dataslayer asserts the algorithm "detects pattern-matching against known AI styles" with no evidence given. VENDOR, unverified. — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/linkedin-algorithm-february-2026-whats-working-now)
- Personal profiles earn 63% higher engagement than company pages. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/press-release-linkedin-study-2026/)
- Posts that spark early discussion outperform those collecting passive likes; 86% of engagements are likes. — [Koka Sexton / SignalScout](https://kokasexton.com/linkedin-algorithm-report-2026/)
- Very airy, one-sentence-per-line formatting gets lower reach than moderate line breaks. — [The Shield Index](https://theshieldindex.beehiiv.com/p/does-linkedin-post-length-actually-matter)
- AI answer engines cite original content 95% of the time versus reshares. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/linkedin-ai-visibility-study/)

### Inferences
- With most long posts flagged as AI-like and a member "AI slop" signal arriving, the practical markers of human writing are things a model cannot supply: named hardware and configurations, the author's own measurements or failure stories, a stated opinion, and plain paragraphs rather than the one-line-stanza template.
- Posts that end with a real technical question (for example a design trade-off) fit the "conversation over broadcast" finding.

### Gaps
- No measured data on which AI networking/HPC creators or post types get the most engagement; I did not survey individual creators. A manual review of top posts from practitioners at NVIDIA, Arista, Broadcom, Cisco and independent analysts would be needed.
- No measured evidence that AI-sounding posts receive less reach or engagement on LinkedIn in 2026.
- No study of specific linguistic "AI tells" was retrieved.

## 5. Which video and graphic formats work for technical explainers, and how does repurposing YouTube perform?

### Takeaway
Carousels and multi-image posts are the best-evidenced graphic formats; video is growing in supply but views per video are falling and median paid watch time is under six seconds, so short, captioned, front-loaded clips are the safe choice. I found no measured data on YouTube-to-LinkedIn repurposing.

### Cited Findings
- Native documents (carousels) have the highest engagement rate (7.00%) and multi-image posts are second (6.45%); video is 6.00%. — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/linkedin-benchmark/)
- Average video views down 36% YoY while pages doubled video posting. — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/linkedin-benchmark/)
- Video now underperforms carousels after outperforming in 2025. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/press-release-linkedin-study-2026/)
- Vidpros compilation (12 Mar 2026), figures secondhand: LinkedIn ad benchmarks show median watch time 5.86 seconds and 60.5% of viewers not completing; short-form shows a 17% lift in completion rate; LinkedIn reported total video viewership up 36% YoY (BACKGROUND, LinkedIn FY25 earnings). — [Vidpros](https://vidpros.com/linkedin-video-statistics/)
- 91% of video is viewed muted (attributed to Sprout Social 2026, secondhand), which makes burned-in captions necessary. — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/linkedin-algorithm-february-2026-whats-working-now)
- Recommended lengths of 30-90 seconds for video and 5-10 slides for carousels are VENDOR guidance without cited data. — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/linkedin-algorithm-february-2026-whats-working-now)
- Long captions hurt document/carousel reach. — [The Shield Index](https://theshieldindex.beehiiv.com/p/does-linkedin-post-length-actually-matter)
- YouTube presence is the strongest correlate of AI Overview visibility, about 23.3% of AIO citations (BACKGROUND 2025). — [Radiant Elephant](https://www.radiantelephant.com/?p=47908)

### Inferences
- For a technical explainer, an architecture diagram broken into a carousel (one idea per slide) with a short caption is the highest-confidence format; a 30-90 second captioned clip that shows the key diagram in the first few seconds is the fallback for video.
- A sensible split: full explainer on YouTube (for Google and AI Overview visibility), a native short cut plus carousel on LinkedIn, and a LinkedIn article with a query-matching headline for search and AI citation. Posting a YouTube link directly would fall into the lowest-performing link format.

### Gaps
- No measured study on repurposed YouTube content versus native LinkedIn video, on vertical versus landscape, or on diagram-style versus talking-head technical video.
- No measured 2026 data on optimal carousel slide count or video length; all figures found are vendor recommendations.
